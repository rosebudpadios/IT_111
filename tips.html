Digital Citizenship Tips

Small, everyday habits can help you become a more responsible digital citizen.

PRACTICAL GUIDELINES

6 Ways to Show Good Digital Character

01

Think Before You Post

Consider how your words or images might affect other people now and in the future.

02

Protect Your Privacy

Use strong passwords and avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly.

03

Check the Facts

Verify information through reliable sources before reposting or forwarding it.

04

Respect Others

Communicate politely, respect differences, and never participate in cyberbullying.

05

Give Proper Credit

Cite sources and respect other people's writing, images, music, and ideas.

06

Report Harmful Content

Use platform reporting tools and seek help from a trusted adult or authority when needed.

YOUR DIGITAL PROMISE

Choose to be a positive influence.

Use technology to learn, create, collaborate, and help others.
