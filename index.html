UNDERSTANDING DIGITAL CHARACTER

What Is Digital Character?

Digital character is how you show your values through the things you say, share, and do online. Every message, comment, and post helps shape your digital footprint.

🤝

Respect

Be kind to others, even when you disagree. Avoid hurtful comments and cyberbullying.

✅

Honesty

Share truthful information, give credit to creators, and avoid plagiarism.

🛡️

Responsibility

Think before posting and protect your personal information and passwords.

A SIMPLE REMINDER

Pause. Think. Post.

Before sharing something online, ask: Is it true? Is it kind? Is it safe?
